Education & School Cleaning

A safe learning environment starts with cleanliness

From classrooms to cafeterias, we help create clean, healthy learning environments for students and staff. Daily sanitation, high-touch disinfection, floor care, and restroom cleaning ensure your campus stays safe and welcoming. We serve schools, campuses, and educational facilities throughout Tyler and East Texas.

What's included

Every plan is customized to your facility, but a typical scope includes:

  • Classroom cleaning & sanitation
  • Cafeteria & common-area cleaning
  • High-touch disinfection (desks, doors, handrails)
  • Restroom cleaning & restocking
  • Gym & hallway floor care
  • Trash & recycling removal
  • Window & entryway cleaning
  • Schedules built around the school day

Why RELI

Protecting students and staff

Every service is held to strict healthcare-grade standards, using hospital-grade disinfectants on the high-touch surfaces — desks, door handles, handrails — where germs spread fastest in a school.

As a veteran-owned and Christian-owned company with over 30 years of experience, our fully vetted, trained team schedules work around the school day so learning is never disrupted.
